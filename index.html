Consecration Protocols

Choose Your Fasting Track

Fasting is not starvation; it is the spiritual posture of feasting on God while muting natural appetites. Select the track that aligns with your health and spiritual capacity.

The Daniel Fast

Daniel 10:2-3

Abstinence from meats, sweets, processed foods, and pleasant wine. Consume vegetables, fruits, legumes, nuts, whole grains, and abundant water throughout the 40 days.

Recommended for All Seekers

Dawn-to-Dusk Fast

6:00 AM – 6:00 PM Daily

Abstain from all solid food from sunrise until 6:00 PM evening watch. Hydrate with water, herbal tea, and break each evening with light nourishing meals.

Most Popular Track

Liquid & Juice Fast

Spiritual Consecration

Consuming only pure water, organic freshly pressed vegetable juices, and broth. Undertaken for specific days (e.g. 3-day or 7-day intervals) during the 40 days.

Requires Physical Stamina

Media & Soul Fast

Sensory Sanctification

Muting secular entertainment, social media scrolls, gossip, and divisive digital noise. Replacing secular consumption with scripture reading and sermons.

Recommended in Tandem

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining Divine Encounter 40.

Is registration completely free?

Yes, 100% free! Divine Encounter 40 is a sacred solemn assembly organized by Divine Connection Ministries Worldwide for global spiritual empowerment. There are no fees to register, participate, or receive the daily devotional guide.

What if I have medical conditions or take daily medications?

Please prioritize your physical health and heed your doctor's guidance. You can participate in the Daniel Fast (nourishing vegetables and whole foods) or join through our Media and Prayer consecration track. God evaluates your heart devotion, not physical harm.

What are the daily prayer shift hours and where do I join?

The program runs from November 11 to December 20, 2026. Daily shifts take place at: Shift 1 (08:30 AM–12:30 PM), Shift 2 (5:00 PM–6:00 PM), and Shift 3 (11:00 PM–1:00 AM). You can join directly via our Zoom Room (Meeting ID: 760 785 7923) or our official YouTube channel @DivineConnectionMGlobal.

How will I receive the daily prayer points?

Once registered, all 40 days of prayer points are accessible right here on this interactive portal in both English and French. We also dispatch daily prayer bulletins with scripture references and broadcast reminders.

Can I fast while working a demanding corporate or manual job?

Yes! Many professionals participate successfully by engaging the Dawn-to-Dusk track (drinking adequate water throughout the day) or the Daniel Fast. You can listen in to the prayer watches during your commute or breaks.
